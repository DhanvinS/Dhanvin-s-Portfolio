01 About

I'm a Machine Learning Engineer focused on AI evaluation and model quality. My work is building the systems that tell teams whether a model actually got better: evaluation frameworks, benchmarks, regression tests, and the infrastructure to run them at scale across LLMs and multimodal systems.

I care about measurement validity: evaluating at capability level instead of a single aggregate number, building failure taxonomies, and catching the ways a benchmark can quietly mislead you, such as label leakage, saturated evals, or optimising a visible proxy over the real objective. I've owned model quality in production over millions of events a day, and I turn research ideas into reproducible, containerised systems other people can use.
